# ADR-0004: Avatar is expression, not authority

## Status

Accepted.

## Context

The final stage presents Concierge as an avatar with voice and camera awareness. This may make the agent feel more human and more authoritative.

## Decision

The avatar expresses interaction stance, but does not own governance or decision authority.

## Consequences

Positive:

- Keeps trust boundary clear
- Reduces manipulation risk
- Preserves Napoleon governance
- Supports adult and child safety profiles

Tradeoffs:

- Requires careful mapping from stance to expression
- Requires audit logs for avatar behavior
