Critique your own Concierge design.

Identify:

1. Missing contracts
2. Unsafe authority assumptions
3. Weak observability
4. Missing adult versus child profile differences
5. Missing stance or tone controls
6. Bad avatar or camera assumptions
7. Missing evaluator cases
8. Weak self-evolution controls
9. Architecture coupling
10. Deployment risks

Then revise the design with concrete changes and regression tests.
